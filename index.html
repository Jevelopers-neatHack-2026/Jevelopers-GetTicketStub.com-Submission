Don'ts

Drop a screenshot, email or PDF to start your stub.

No body text in orange, copper or cyan on cream. They fail 4.5:1. Use ink.

No bare stamp on busy art. Use the mono stamp on a cream or ink plate.

No rotating, re-colouring or effects on the mark. The foil is always copper → orange → cyan at 135°.
