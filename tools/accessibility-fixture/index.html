Pairing restrictions

  • Use ink labels on orange controls. Cream labels on orange fail normal-text contrast.
  • Orange, copper and cyan are prohibited for body text on cream. Copper on ink also fails normal-text contrast.
  • Keep the cyan focus outline separated from orange by the surrounding background. Cyan directly on orange fails 3:1.
  • Tint and moss alone do not establish meaningful input boundaries. These samples add measured text-color borders.
  • Place essential text on an opaque measured plate, not directly on gradients or busy artwork.
  • Use text, shape and an accessible name as well as color to communicate status.

Logotype and decorative exceptions do not excuse inaccessible functional labels. Application owners remain responsible for their actual layouts, keyboard flows, imagery and runtime motion.
