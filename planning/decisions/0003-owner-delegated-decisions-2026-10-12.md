| K1 | **Tombstone retention: at most 7 years (2,555 days) after creation. `retiredKeyRetentionMs = 220838400000` (2,556 days, 7 years + 1 day).** Other policy values stay at their defaults: 90-day rotation, 180-day max key age, 5-min skew, 64 versions. | **No approved tombstone retention policy exists** in fulfillment-connectors, shared-contracts or getticketstub.com (`security.md` and `key-management.md` say the package "invents no retention period"). A tombstone must outlive every way a deleted purchase action could come back: supplier idempotency windows (30 days in the simulator), card disputes (months) and the 7-year US record-keeping horizon that the finance ledger keeps. 4. A sandbox adapter change. Order create currently sends no `callbackUrl`.
