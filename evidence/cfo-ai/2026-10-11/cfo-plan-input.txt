# TicketStub — Jevelopers / neatHack business plan

Prepared 2026-10-11. Currency: USD. Planning model, not a record of actual sales. User authorized reasonable guesses for opening cash and founder pay; neither is measured. Use $1,000 hypothetical opening cash and $0 cash founder pay for the bootstrap illustration, with $0/$5,000 cash and $3,000 monthly founder-pay sensitivities. No private balance or personal financial information is included.

## Product, customer and commercial strategy

TicketStub turns an event link and personal memories into a researched, editable commemorative ticket design. The studio supports styles, artwork remix, fact review, personal details, sharing, a wall, matching sets, gifting and organizer flows. Target customers are concert, sports and theatre attendees who want a keepsake, and gift buyers. Event organizers are a later distribution channel; no signed partnerships, validated demand, market size or organizer revenue is claimed.

The proposed launch is a $9 digital keepsake sold per design, not a subscription and not an admission ticket. Start with one event/fan community, demonstrate the editing journey, and test willingness to pay through organizer referrals and customer sharing. Track visits, studio starts, paid conversion, per-attempt usage, revisions, refunds and repeat purchases. Digital delivery itself still needs production acceptance. Compete on event-specific provenance and personalization against generic design tools, screenshots and commodity print shops; differentiation and demand remain hypotheses.

Sandbox tiers in source are Digital $9, Card $29, Print $49, Framed $89. Physical carts add $6 estimated shipping; foil +$6 and holo +$9 are sandbox upsells, omitted from revenue forecasts because supplier support/cost is unknown. These are starting price hypotheses, not validated live prices. No organizer discount or subscription revenue is assumed.

Current main is a demo prototype: external calls disabled by default; checkout, payments, shipping, refunds, wallet passes and sign-in are simulated/sandbox. Browser storage and payload share links are not production persistence. The separate PR #20 has proposed identity/persistence changes but is not treated as accepted main functionality. Payment provider implementation, live production controls and supplier SKU validation remain gates. Creative-engine has local injected interfaces and synthetic checks; fulfillment-connectors has no live Prodigi adapter, accepted SKU or supplier quote. Cost-intelligence's synthetic tariff is not a current vendor quote. No working CFO API sync is claimed or needed for this plan.

Current vendor rates checked 2026-10-11 UTC through unauthenticated official catalog endpoints:

- OpenRouter text catalog: `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5` research and `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` chat each list $2 per million input and $10 per million output tokens. Cache rates are listed but no caching benefit is assumed. GPT long-context overrides are irrelevant to the assumed 3,000-token chat input. Research does not invoke paid search tools.
- OpenRouter dedicated image endpoint for `openai/gpt-image-2`: text input $5/M tokens, image input $8/M tokens, image output $30/M tokens. These are token rates, not a flat image price. App requests one output with provider defaults, so quality/size and actual billed image tokens remain unknown. Budget 150 text-input + 4,000 image-output tokens per generation = $0.12075 (estimate). Image-input tokens are zero for current text-only generation; a true image edit would add billed input tokens. Each extra 4,000 image-input tokens adds $0.032 before the funding allowance.
- Stripe US standard domestic-card benchmark: 2.9% + $0.30 per successful transaction. US entity/domestic cards are planning assumptions; processor, jurisdiction, taxes, cross-border/FX fees and dispute fees remain unconfirmed. This benchmark does not establish a Stripe integration.
- OpenRouter FAQ initially omitted dynamic fee values in its text rendering. A later independent check of the official HTML verified card-funded credits at 5.5% with a $0.80 minimum. Retain a 6% inference-funding allowance as a conservative ESTIMATE, distinct from the published vendor fee; sensitivity 0–10%. The minimum fee can exceed this allowance on small top-ups (e.g. $0.80 on $10 = 8%). Credit deposits are prepaid cash, not inference expense a second time.

Estimates, not vendor quotes: all traffic/conversion, tokens/request, generation/revision counts, fixed expenses, marketing, support, refund allowances, printing, shipping, packing, launch contingency, payout timing, opening cash and founder pay. Unknowns include actual usage, tax jurisdiction, legal/entity costs, insurance, paid event-data licenses, labor burden, supplier prices, SKU/specifications, commercial weather terms and all production bills. Do not silently set unknowns to measured zero.

## AI cost and funnel formulas

Research/start: one call, 4,000 input + 600 output = $0.014. Chat call: 3,000 input + 300 output = $0.009. Source route output caps are 600 research / 300 chat; assumed inputs are not measured. Image generation includes initial artwork and all subsequent regenerations. Conservative 4 = 1 initial + 3 revisions; Base 2 = 1 initial + 1 revision; Growth 1.5 = 1 initial + 0.5 expected revision. Chats/start 8 / 4 / 3 respectively. No automatic retry is implemented in the app; any future separately billed retry must count as another attempt.

Visits/month 5,000 / 10,000 / 25,000. Studio-start rate 10% of visits in all cases. Paid conversion is 1% / 2% / 4% of visits, so 50 / 200 / 1,000 orders and 500 / 1,000 / 2,500 studio starts. These are post-launch hypotheses, not a traction claim. One design/order and one charge/order; basket effects excluded. Charge every studio start, including abandoned sessions, at its scenario's full average usage (a cautious simplifying assumption).

AI/start = (0.014 + chats×0.009 + generations×0.12075)×1.06.
AI/order = AI/start×studio-start-rate/paid-conversion.
Orders = visits×paid-conversion. Monthly AI = visits×start-rate×AI/start.
Digital contribution/order = $9 − ($9×2.9%+$0.30) − $9×2% refund allowance − $0.20 variable operations − AI/order.
The $0.20 includes $0.15 support cash allowance, $0.03 storage/egress/delivery and $0.02 email; human founder time is not included here. Original payment fees are retained in refund cases. The allowance reduces net contribution/cash, not the count of booked orders.
Operating profit = orders×contribution/order − fixed overhead − marketing. These are simplified cash operating results before income tax, depreciation, founder pay and unpriced launch/legal costs, not GAAP statements.
Base fixed-traffic break-even conversion is approximately 1.128% of 10,000 visits, holding 1,000 charged studio starts and $600 overhead/marketing constant. This differs from scaling traffic at constant conversion.

Break-even orders = ceiling((fixed+marketing)/contribution/order), holding conversion, usage, channel mix and costs constant. Fixed-traffic conversion sensitivity must instead charge the same studio spend across fewer/more purchases. Neither break-even formula guarantees attainable demand.

## Physical economics and launch gates

Exclude all physical revenue from all three forecasts. Conditional single-item matte order: add $6 customer shipping to item price; supplier printing Card/Print/Framed $5/$12/$35, actual shipping $6/$8/$12, packing $1/$2/$3 (all estimates). Deduct payment fees on total receipt, 5% total-receipt refund/reprint allowance, $0.50 variable support/delivery and Base AI/order. Shipping charged and shipping paid appear separately. A 25% increase in printing+shipping+packing reduces contribution by $3/$5.50/$12.50 respectively. Supplier tax, duties and remote destinations can add more.

Validate SKU, finish, dimensions, print asset quality, destination, shipping method, quotes, lead times, tax, defects/refunds, sandbox order/reconciliation and production authorization before taking real physical payment. Foil/holo support is not assumed. For a hypothetical 100 Card orders/month, supplier print+shipping+packing is $1,200; a 14-day prepayment/payout timing gap requires $560 working capital, plus a $175 (5% receipts) reprint/refund reserve. This $735 physical cash buffer is separate from the digital model and is not proof of supplier terms.

## Cash, runway and risks

Opening cash: unknown; use hypothetical $1,000 for bootstrap review, alternatives $0/$5,000. Founder pay: $0 cash bootstrap assumption, plus $3,000/month sensitivity (payroll burden unknown). $0 salary does not mean free labor or a viable full-time income. With Base 200 orders, $702.81 monthly surplus before founder pay becomes a $2,297.19 deficit after $3,000 pay.

Launch reserve = $500 one-off contingency + 7/30 of monthly variable costs (estimated seven-day payout float). Runway = max(0, opening cash − reserve)/monthly burn for a cash-burning scenario. When steady state is cash-positive, report that fact without claiming infinite runway: reserve, ramp-up, taxes and adverse events still require cash. Six-month cash need = reserve + six months of positive burn, with/without founder pay. Prelaunch Base fixed+marketing burn is $600/month, or $3,600 including founder pay; each prelaunch month adds this amount to funding needs. No prizes or credits fund this reserve. Upfront inference deposits may require extra float beyond consumed costs, depending on purchase size.

A 3-month Base prelaunch plus 6-month Base postlaunch at $3,000 founder pay requires approximately $25,199.15 (3×$3,600 + $616.01 launch/float reserve + 6×$2,297.19), before taxes and unknown legal costs. Bootstrap Base needs $2,416.01 for 3 prelaunch months plus its launch reserve, even though steady-state postlaunch contribution is positive.

Key risks: conversion below assumptions; free-user abuse; more image revisions; image default token costs; seasonality; rights/licensing and event fact errors; refund/dispute losses; unpaid labor; unvalidated fulfillment quality; missing production identity, payments, persistence and recovery controls. Use free procedural previews, paid generation gates or bounded quotas, usage receipts and per-project budgets as proposed mitigations (not all implemented). Do not claim free tiers support commercial production. Recheck rates, sampled bills, physical quotes and pricing before launch.

Validation sequence: review this price/cost hypothesis; measure a small authorized design cohort including abandoned attempts; test willingness to pay and conversion; qualify digital production delivery/payment controls; independently validate supplier/SKU and quote each destination before physical sales. No paid experiments or changes in service billing are authorized by this plan.
