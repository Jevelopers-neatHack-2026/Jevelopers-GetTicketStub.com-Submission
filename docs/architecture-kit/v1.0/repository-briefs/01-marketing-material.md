# `marketing-material`

**Repository status:** Existing name supplied by the team.  
**Proposed owner:** Product and frontend.  
**Delivery state:** Planning only; all checks pending.

## Responsibility

Own the words, examples, and presentation materials that explain the commemorative-ticket product. Keep public claims aligned with demonstrated behavior and the evidence assembled for submission. The repository should make it easy to produce a coherent product introduction, short demonstration, and team handoff from the same approved messages.

This repository consumes brand assets and technical evidence. It does not own visual identity, application code, financial calculations, or authoritative hackathon requirements.

## Planned internal modules

- `messaging/`: Product positioning, audience, value proposition, and approved claims.
- `demo/`: Storyboard, narration, recording checklist, and evidence references.
- `launch/`: Landing-page copy, social drafts, and product descriptions.
- `examples/`: Approved demonstration outputs with permission and attribution notes.
- `review/`: Content review records and the source behind each measurable claim.

## Dependencies and handoff

Use versioned identity guidance from `brand-kit`, product behavior from `getticketstub.com`, and sanitized measurements from `integration-evals`. Financial statements must match the reporting definitions in `cost-intelligence`. Draft content should identify its intended destination and reviewer before publication.

## P0 demo slice

Prepare one product explanation, one complete demonstration script, and a compact submission narrative. Describe simulated transactions and forecast assumptions clearly. Check the current official submission format before producing final media.

## Acceptance boundary

- [ ] Demonstrated capabilities and future features are distinguishable.
- [ ] Measured claims link to reproducible evidence or labeled assumptions.
- [ ] Included artwork and screenshots are approved for the intended use.
- [ ] No customer details, secrets, or private financial account data appear.
- [ ] Final copy follows the approved brand vocabulary.
