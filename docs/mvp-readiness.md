## What is available on main

The [contract catalog](contract-catalog.md) remains the descriptive interface source. It is not a generated package manifest and does not mean that every proposed contract has an accepted schema version. Main also contains two separately scoped tools:

- The [consumer receipt metadata checker](receipt-checker.md) checks required fields, pin and digest formats, internal consistency, and bounded input handling. It never executes the recorded commands, authenticates a reviewer or source, or grants acceptance.
- The [reporting proposal packet](../proposals/reporting-v1/README.md) contains two JSON Schema 2020-12 proposals and synthetic examples for Finance review. Its offline validator uses externally provisioned Ajv 8.17.1; it does not install dependencies or promote the schemas into the canonical interface set.

The documented 0.9.7, 0.10.1, and 0.11.1 candidates are all distinct from main. Each candidate has its own source, archive, fixture, and review evidence. Never reuse a receipt across package versions or changed archive bytes. The 0.9.8 Finance caller receipt is also tied to its own exact private artifact and does not transfer to another candidate.

## Release blockers

The live gate and the linked immutable receipts retain these material gaps:

- the required 32-leaf implementation/review scope is not closed, including the open leaf issues #32 and #34–#37;
- the 76 catalog names still lack named canonical owner/reviewer acknowledgements;
- only 3 of 9 consumers have scoped private-development receipts, and those do not establish domain-owner acceptance or the product journey;
- Finance has not adopted a reviewed shared-contracts pin; its existing 0.3.0 archive remains marked proposal_only;
- the local-usage pre-ingestion/delivery/receipt mapping remains unresolved ([#11](https://github.com/Jevelopers-neatHack-2026/shared-contracts/issues/11));
- the legacy Preview/1 to ArtifactFile/2 approval identity mapping remains unresolved ([#22](https://github.com/Jevelopers-neatHack-2026/shared-contracts/issues/22));
- production CSV input, draft-row exposure, and repair lineage require separately versioned owner decisions ([#36](https://github.com/Jevelopers-neatHack-2026/shared-contracts/issues/36)); the newer production-boundary candidate also has blocking review findings;
- GAP-001 reporting and GAP-002 allowed-work/replanning remain open. The merged PR #45 proposal is review input only and does not close GAP-001.

These are source-backed status statements, not a substitute for the full issue criteria. A merged proposal, passing local tests, or completed consumer fixture does not close an issue or the SC-12 release gate.
