# Voice

**Rule: clarity over cleverness. Lead with what the user gets, then tell them exactly what to do next.**

**All product wording on this page is illustrative and pending review.** It demonstrates tone, including planned features, unverified sample event data and sample prices. It does not establish supported inputs, complete automation, a working email flow, available products or fulfillment. [Claim records](claims-register.md) identify the exact examples, required evidence and reviewer roles. There is no approved public wording in this guide until the product and MM-02 claim owners record it.

The product is playful; the words are not. Delight comes from the stub, the sound and the tear. Copy keeps the path to a finished stub obvious and fast.

## Principles

1. **Benefit first, action second.** "Get a stub you'll want to frame. Drop your ticket." Say what they get before what they do.
2. **One action per screen.** Each screen has one primary verb. Secondary actions are quieter and fewer.
3. **Buttons are verbs.** Use "Print it", "Save to your wall", "Tear to finish". Never "Submit", "OK" or "Continue".
4. **Specific beats vague.** Use verified names and approved prices when they exist. "Framed print, $89" is an illustrative price example for review, not an offer; a published price needs currency, scope and current product/fulfillment evidence.
5. **Short.** Titles and buttons are at most 8 words, and body text at most 2 sentences.
6. **Plain words.** Say "screenshot", "ticket", "stub" and "wall", not "OCR", "template", "asset" or "collection item".
7. **No puns, no exclamation marks, no "Oops".** Errors say what happened and how to fix it.
8. **Sentence case** everywhere except stub artwork (each edition sets its own case).

Stub content (facts printed on the stub) is different from UI copy. "Night 2 of 3 · 7°C · Rain" is data and stays terse.

**Illustrative examples only.** Keep this label with copied screenshots or excerpts. A copy style preference cannot substantiate a capability; the [claim register](claims-register.md) retains the original examples and their public-use blockers.

## Claim review handoff

For a proposed public sentence, link the exact rendered copy and revision, supported input/feature modes, limits and recovery paths, product evidence, and the dated MM-02 wording decision. Price, shipping and sponsor wording also needs the corresponding accountable commerce or partnership evidence. Unknown values remain unknown; do not infer an offer from the price ladder above.

Negative review fixture: presenting **"No forms: drop your ticket and it's done"** without the illustrative label and supported-input/recovery evidence must be rejected. The valid documentation alternative is **"Illustrative planned experience: drop a ticket to start a stub"** with the pending claim record attached. Neither wording is approved for a live product claim here.
